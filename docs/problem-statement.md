# Problem Statement

## Current Problem

Most existing learning platforms focus on content delivery. They provide courses, videos, and materials but do not understand individual student behavior.

Students often face problems:

- They cannot identify their weak topics.
- They do not know what to study next.
- They cannot create an effective study plan.
- Teachers cannot easily monitor individual learning difficulties.

## Proposed Solution

LearnLens AI analyzes:

- Quiz performance
- Assignment results
- Study activities
- Learning behavior

Using Artificial Intelligence, the system generates:

- Personalized learning recommendations
- Weak topic detection
- Adaptive study plans
- Learning progress insights
> **Project Vision Note:**
 Designed to bridge individual knowledge gaps through Socratic evaluation and adaptive AI feedback loops.
